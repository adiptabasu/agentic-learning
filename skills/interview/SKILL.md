# Mock interview

Use this skill to practice an employment, academic, or other structured interview. For a subject quiz, diagnostic, or exam-readiness check, use `skills/assessment/SKILL.md` instead.

## Prepare

- Clarify the role or subject, level, interview type, relevant job description or criteria, and preferred feedback timing only where these affect the questions.
- If requirements are current or organization-specific, verify from authoritative sources when possible and state what is verified versus assumed.
- Explain the format and rubric before starting. Ask whether the learner wants coaching after each answer or a full uninterrupted interview followed by feedback.
- Do not create or save a question bank unless the learner has agreed on its path, contents, and Git policy.

## Conduct the interview

- Ask one question at a time by default. Wait for the learner's answer; do not disclose a model answer, key, or rubric details that would spoil the attempt.
- Use realistic follow-ups based on what the learner actually says. Keep prompts relevant to the role and avoid pretending a generated prompt is an official employer question.
- Assess answers against explicit, relevant criteria. Separate subject knowledge, reasoning, and communication when useful. Do not infer experience, credentials, or accomplishments.
- If an agreed topic bank is configured, use `skills/question-bank/SKILL.md` to save the substantive questions and follow-ups asked, with role context and scoring rubrics, under the agreed policy. If no bank exists, propose setup and keep entries in the conversation until its location and Git policy are agreed. Keep attempts and feedback separate from the reusable bank.
- Explain that practice provides limited evidence and does not guarantee hiring or overall readiness.

## Close interview mode

Summarize strengths tied to the answers, areas to practice, uncertainties, and focused next steps. Summarize which question-bank entries were saved or still need a configured destination. Do not save interview attempts without opt-in. Then state that Interview mode is closed.
