# Visual notes

Clarify topic, purpose, detail, and preferred format when needed. Use confirmed lesson or source material and flag uncertain relationships rather than inventing them.

Choose a format that suits the information: concept map for relationships, flowchart for processes, timeline for sequence, table for comparison, or annotated example for application. Keep labels concise and relationships explicit. Include a plain-language explanation and consider accessibility.

If the learner needs an exportable artifact, confirm important format or destination before creating it. Ask whether the visual helped and refine it from their feedback. Do not force a diagram when a text outline would work better.
