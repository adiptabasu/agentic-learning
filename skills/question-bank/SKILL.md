# Question bank

Use this skill to set up, maintain, or practice from an agreed bank. Assessment and interview workflows hand question storage and retrieval to this skill.

## Consent and placement

- Before creating a bank, agree on its contents, exact paths, and Git policy. Adapt to an existing workspace rather than imposing a layout. The learner wants substantive assessment and interview questions preserved; once the active topic bank and save policy are agreed, add questions from those sessions by default unless they opt out for a session.
- For multiple topics, a small `QUESTION-BANK.md` index may link to topic-specific banks beside the relevant materials. On resume, read the index and only the active topic bank and its relevant sources by default.
- Do not save learner responses, hints used, scores, or confidence in the bank. Those are personal attempt history and require a separate opt-in location such as an agreed journal.

## Curate reusable questions

- Keep questions grounded in material taught, assigned, or relevant to the agreed interview/exam target. Preserve each substantive assessment/interview question asked, with its relevant follow-ups, rather than every incidental conversational check or hint.
- Include a stable ID, topic/objective, type, difficulty if useful, prompt, optional progressive hints, answer key or acceptable answer elements/rubric, explanation, and source references. For interviews, include role/level/context, follow-up prompts, and an explicit scoring rubric where appropriate.
- Keep expected answers hidden during practice until the learner has tried or asks to see them. Separate assessment/recall questions from interview prompts in bank metadata.
- Distinguish generated practice from official released questions. Verify current exam or role requirements from authoritative sources when they may have changed; mark uncertain entries for review.
- Check for duplicates and avoid answer keys stated as certain when source evidence is incomplete or disputed.
- When setting up a bank or changing its structure, show the proposed files and policy before saving. Once an agreed bank policy authorizes routine question capture, save each session's substantive questions and keys/rubrics without asking again each time; summarize what was added. Otherwise show proposed additions for review before saving. Write only to agreed files and preserve unrelated content.

## Practice and close

- Select from the active topic bank only. Ask one question at a time by default and do not reveal the answer key or rubric before the response unless requested.
- Give feedback tied to the saved key/rubric and source. One answer is evidence about that item, not proof of broad mastery.
- When the learner ends Question Bank mode, finish the requested curation or practice, summarize what was saved or left unsaved and where, and surface any entry that needs learner correction. Then state that the mode is closed.
