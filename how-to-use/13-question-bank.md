# Keep a topic-based question bank

Use this to preserve reusable assessment and interview questions for practice over time. Agree on the bank's location, contents, and Git policy before creating personal files. Once the bank is configured, substantive questions from those sessions are saved under that policy. Interview prompts may include follow-ups and scoring rubrics; topic checks may include keyed answers and explanations.

## Set up the bank

Ask the agent to propose a small central index and topic banks beside their learning materials:

> Use `skills/question-bank/SKILL.md` to set up a topic-based question bank in my learner-selected workspace. Propose a central `QUESTION-BANK.md` index and banks at [topic paths] beside the relevant materials. Use the blank templates if helpful. Include routine saving of substantive assessment and interview questions after setup. Tell me exactly what will be stored and whether it will be tracked in Git. Do not create files until I agree to the paths and policy.

The central index links to topic banks; it should not duplicate their questions. You can choose a different structure that fits an existing repository.

## Add questions as you learn

After an assessment, lesson, or mock interview, ask for a small, useful selection rather than a transcript of every practice prompt:

> From today's assessment or interview, save the substantive questions to [topic]'s configured bank. Label each as an assessment or interview question. Include the learning objective, type or difficulty, optional hints, answer key or interview rubric, explanation, and source. Mark uncertain answers for review. Summarize what you added. Do not record my attempts.

Objective questions can have a keyed answer and explanation. Open-ended or interview questions should use acceptable answer elements or a scoring rubric. Distinguish original practice from official released questions.

## Practice from the bank

> Quiz me from [active topic]'s bank on [topic or objectives]. Ask one question at a time and do not show the answer key before I try. Give hints only if I ask. After each answer, explain the result using the key or rubric and tell me what to review. Do not save my attempt unless I ask.

When a question depends on changing requirements or current exam formats, ask the agent to verify authoritative sources and note when they were checked. A bank answer is not automatically current just because it was saved earlier.

## Track progress separately

Question prompts and answer keys or rubrics belong in the topic bank. Learner responses, hint use, scores, and review dates are personal progress data; save those separately in the agreed topic journal or attempt log only if you want them recorded. One correct answer is evidence about that question, not proof of mastery.

When you finish Question Bank mode, review the summary of what was saved and where, and correct any inaccurate entry. The mode should close with a brief status; it should not silently save learner attempts.
