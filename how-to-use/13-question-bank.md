# Keep a topic-based question bank

Use this when you want useful questions and answer keys from lessons to remain available for practice over time. A bank is optional. Agree on its location, contents, and Git policy before creating personal files.

## Set up the bank

Ask the agent to propose a small central index and topic banks beside their learning materials:

> Use `skills/assessment/SKILL.md` to set up an optional topic-based question bank in my learner-selected workspace. Propose a central `QUESTION-BANK.md` index and banks at [topic paths] beside the relevant materials. Use the blank templates if helpful. Tell me exactly what will be stored and whether it will be tracked in Git. Do not create files until I agree to the paths and policy.

The central index links to topic banks; it should not duplicate their questions. You can choose a different structure that fits an existing repository.

## Add questions as you learn

After a lesson, ask for a small, useful selection rather than a transcript of every practice prompt:

> From today's covered material, propose [number] reusable questions for [topic]'s bank. Include the learning objective, type or difficulty, optional hints, answer key or rubric, explanation, and source. Mark uncertain answers for review. Show me the additions before saving them to [agreed path]. Do not record my attempts.

Objective questions can have a keyed answer and explanation. Open-ended or interview questions should use acceptable answer elements or a scoring rubric. Distinguish original practice from official released questions.

## Practice from the bank

> Quiz me from [active topic]'s bank on [topic or objectives]. Ask one question at a time and do not show the answer key before I try. Give hints only if I ask. After each answer, explain the result using the key or rubric and tell me what to review. Do not save my attempt unless I ask.

When a question depends on changing requirements or current exam formats, ask the agent to verify authoritative sources and note when they were checked. A bank answer is not automatically current just because it was saved earlier.

## Track progress separately

Question prompts and answer keys belong in the topic bank. Learner responses, hint use, scores, and review dates are personal progress data; save those separately in the agreed topic journal or attempt log only if you want them recorded. One correct answer is evidence about that question, not proof of mastery.
