# Learn a topic in one session

Use this when you want to understand a specific topic now. You do not need a long-term plan, journal, or workspace to start learning.

## Start a focused lesson

Invoke `teacher` if available, or say:

> Read `skills/teacher/SKILL.md` and `personas/teacher.md` and follow them. Teach me [topic]. I have [time] and want to be able to [specific result, if known]. Start with a short explanation and example, then give me a chance to try. Ask about my study preferences only if they would change how you teach.

Example:

> Read `skills/teacher/SKILL.md` and follow it. Teach me recursion. I have about 20 minutes. Explain it with a simple example, then give me a small problem to solve. Don't show me the answer before I try.

You can also say what you already know or where you get stuck. If you don't know your level, say so; the Teacher should begin with a useful explanation and adjust from your responses.

## During the lesson

Try the practice question before asking for the answer. If you are stuck, ask for a hint:

> Give me one small hint without revealing the full solution.

If the explanation isn't working, be specific:

> I lost track at [step or idea]. Can you explain it another way, using [a different example / a diagram / simpler terms]?

Ask for a quick understanding check:

> Give me one new example to solve so we can check whether I can apply the idea, not just repeat your example.

## Close the session

Ask the Teacher to summarize:

> Summarize what we covered, what my answers showed I understand, and what remains unclear. Suggest one useful next step.

You can stop there. A journal is optional. If you want to continue this topic in a future session, choose a workspace and agree on what to save before asking the agent to create or update records.
