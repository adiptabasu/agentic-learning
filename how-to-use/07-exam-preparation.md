# Prepare for an exam or qualification

Use this for an exam, certification, entrance test, or other formal qualification. The agent can help you understand requirements, plan study, teach topics, and assess readiness. It cannot guarantee a result.

## Identify the exact exam

Invoke `learning-guide` or say:

> Read `skills/learning-guide/SKILL.md` and follow it. I am preparing for [full exam or qualification name] in [country/region or exam body], scheduled for [date, if known]. Help me clarify my current level, available study time, constraints, and what passing requires. Ask one question at a time.

If the exam format or requirements may have changed, ask for official information:

> Verify the current exam structure, topics, and requirements from the official exam provider. Give me the source links and the date checked. Mark anything you cannot verify.

## Agree on the preparation plan

After the goal summary is accurate, say:

> Use `skills/learning-plan/SKILL.md` to build a preparation plan based on the verified exam requirements and my starting point. Include topic order, practice, milestone checks, and a final review period. Estimate only what my available time supports. Tell me directly if the exam date looks unrealistic.

Review and adjust the plan before you accept it. Then begin teaching using the [goal-to-first-lesson guide](00-start-to-first-lesson.md) or resume with the [pull-and-resume guide](04-resume-after-pull.md).

## Check a topic or simulate a test

For a topic check, say:

> Use `skills/assessment/SKILL.md` to assess me on [topics I have covered]. Ask one question at a time. Don't show the answer before I try. Afterward, explain the scoring and what I should review. Treat this as evidence about these topics, not proof of overall mastery.

For timed practice, specify the conditions:

> Give me an unofficial practice set for [exam/topic] with [number] questions and [time limit]. Match the current format only where verified. Tell me whether the questions are original practice or official released questions. Save the answer key until I finish.

Use the results to adjust the learning plan. Ask for a new set if you want another attempt; repeating the same answers may test memory rather than transfer.

## Keep the record current if you chose a journal

Ask the agent to propose an update in the relevant topic journal with the result, rubric or scoring basis, errors, demonstrated strengths, and review priorities. Update the central index only if something applies across topics. Review the update before it is saved or included in Git. See the [progress journal guide](05-progress-journal.md).
