# Practice for a job interview

Use this to practice for a specific role. A mock interview can help reveal strengths and areas to work on, but it cannot predict or guarantee a hiring outcome.

## Give the interview context

Invoke `assessment` or say:

> Read `skills/assessment/SKILL.md` and follow it. Run a mock interview for a [level] [role] interview at [company or industry, if relevant]. Here is the job description or the main requirements: [paste relevant text]. Ask one question at a time and wait for my answer. Include realistic follow-ups based on what I say.

If you do not have a job description, start with:

> I don't have a specific job description. Ask me the role, level, and interview type you need to know, then use common expectations only where they are reasonable. Mark assumptions clearly.

## Choose how feedback works

Before starting, choose whether you want feedback after each answer or at the end:

> Give me feedback after each answer. Use a clear rubric for technical accuracy, reasoning, and communication. Ask follow-ups naturally, then tell me what worked and what I should improve.

Or:

> Run the whole interview first without coaching between questions. At the end, give me a rubric-based review and show an example of how I could strengthen the weakest answer without inventing experience.

Be truthful about your own experience. If you have not done something, practice explaining your related experience or how you would approach it; do not fabricate projects or credentials.

## Review and repeat

After the interview, ask:

> Separate feedback on my subject knowledge from feedback on communication. Identify the specific answers that support your assessment, what remains uncertain, and three focused practice steps. Do not claim this predicts whether I will get hired.

If useful, repeat with different questions or a different interview type. Do not just memorize polished answers; practice explaining the reasoning in your own words.

If you have a learning plan or journal, ask the agent to propose updates based on the interview evidence and review them before saving. See the [plan](02-plan-for-a-goal.md) and [journal](05-progress-journal.md) guides.
