# Plan toward a specific goal and deadline

Use this when you know the target, such as an exam date, a work project, or a role you want to qualify for, and need a realistic learning route.

## Start with your target

Invoke `learning-guide` or say:

> Read `skills/learning-guide/SKILL.md` and follow it. My target is [specific outcome] by [date, if fixed]. I can study about [time] and I currently know [starting point, if known]. Help me clarify the requirements, my baseline, and constraints before proposing a plan. Ask one question at a time.

Example:

> Read `skills/learning-guide/SKILL.md` and follow it. I want to pass [exam name] by [date]. I can study on weekends for about [hours]. I have [experience or prior preparation]. Find out what my current level is and whether this timeline is realistic before planning.

For a changing exam, credential, or career requirement, expect the agent to check current authoritative sources. If it doesn't, ask:

> Please verify the current official requirements and exam format. Tell me when and where you checked, and distinguish those facts from your estimates about my preparation.

## Confirm the starting point and feasibility

Answer questions about what you have studied, what you can already do, available time, constraints, and the result you need. Review the agent's summary. If you disagree, correct it before continuing. Ask for a candid feasibility assessment:

> Assess whether this target and timeline look feasible given my baseline and available study time. Explain the evidence, assumptions, and unknowns. If the timeline is unlikely, say so and suggest realistic alternatives.

## Request and review the plan

After confirming the goal summary, ask for the route:

> The summary is accurate. Use `skills/learning-plan/SKILL.md` to propose an ordered plan for [target] by [date]. Include prerequisites, weekly or milestone-sized work, practice, checkpoints, evidence of readiness, and review time. Label estimates and flag any trade-offs. Do not guarantee that I will pass or qualify.

Review the workload and milestones. To adjust it, say:

> Revise the plan for [updated weekly time / new constraint]. Keep [priority] central, and show what moves later or is left out.

When it fits, confirm:

> I agree to this plan. Prepare a handoff to the Teacher with the first milestone, my current baseline, the evidence we will use to track progress, and any unresolved risks.

Then use the Teacher as shown in the [goal-to-first-lesson walkthrough](00-start-to-first-lesson.md). Revisit the plan if your schedule or evidence changes; a plan is a working route, not a promise of an outcome.
